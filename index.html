Comprehensive Training and Starting Gradually

Comprehensive training includes strengthening muscles, improving fitness, and increasing flexibility, which are the foundation of building a healthy and balanced body.
It is important to start gradually to avoid injuries and increase the intensity of exercises progressively according to the body's capacity and endurance.

Tips on Diastasis Recti

Diastasis recti is a common change during pregnancy, occurring due to the stretching of abdominal muscles to accommodate the growing baby.
It is important to avoid exercises that increase pressure on the abdomen and focus on deep breathing exercises and gently strengthening the inner muscles under the supervision of a specialist.

Disc: Symptoms and Care Methods

A disc occurs due to a herniated disc between the vertebrae of the spine, which may cause back or limb pain and difficulty in movement.
Caring for it requires adherence to physical therapy, avoiding incorrect movements, and practicing exercises that support spinal health.     

Knee Care and Osteoarthritis Issues

The knee is one of the vital joints that bears a significant load in our daily lives, and neglecting it directly affects the quality of movement.
Taking care of it includes preventing osteoarthritis through appropriate exercises, maintaining weight, and regular medical follow-ups to avoid complications.
