Clinical Services

We offer a variety of therapeutic services designed to promote healing and improve quality of life.

Therapeutic Exercises

Customized exercises designed to improve mobility, flexibility, and strength for a healthier lifestyle.

Educational Sessions

Expert-led sessions to educate patients on injury prevention, rehabilitation, and self-care techniques.

Ergonomic Sessions

Workplace and posture assessments to reduce strain, prevent injuries, and enhance productivity.

Lifestyle Enhancement

Comprehensive programs to improve overall wellness, focusing on fitness, diet, and mental well-being.

Dry Needles Service

A targeted therapy using fine needles to relieve muscle tension, pain, and enhance mobility.

Taping Service

Supportive taping techniques to stabilize joints, reduce pain, and improve muscle function.

Shock Wave Therapy

Non-invasive treatment using sound waves to accelerate healing and pain relief.

Muscle Electrical Stimulation

Advanced electrical therapy to enhance muscle function and reduce pain.

Deep Tissue Release

Manual therapy techniques to release muscle tightness and improve movement.

Blood Flow Restriction (BFR)

A specialized technique for improving strength and recovery with minimal stress on joints.

Running Technique Enhancement

Specialized programs designed to analyze and improve running technique, reducing injury risk and enhancing efficiency.

Personal Training Sessions

One-on-one training sessions tailored to individual fitness goals, improving strength, endurance, and overall health.

Movement Therapy Sessions

Therapeutic exercises focusing on improving body movement, flexibility, and coordination for injury prevention.

Force-Plate Measurements

Advanced biomechanical assessments using force plates to measure balance, strength, and movement patterns.

Strength Measurements

Precision tests to evaluate muscular strength and endurance, aiding in personalized training and rehabilitation plans.

Teletherapy Sessions

Remote physiotherapy consultations offering expert guidance and rehabilitation plans via digital platforms.
